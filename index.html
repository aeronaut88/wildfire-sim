Each tick, every burning cell tries to ignite each neighbor with P = spread × fuel factor × wind factor. Grass catches fast and burns out quickly. Big trees resist ignition but burn for a long time. Burning trees can throw embers downwind. Towns rally once fire comes within sight: crews dig firebreaks, engines spray water, and a rare air tanker drops retardant. They don't always win. Between fires the forest grows back, towns expand along their roads until they hit the size cap, build stations and buy engines, and a prosperous town may open an airstrip. Weather drifts between clear, drought, rain and storms, and storms bring lightning. Settlers wander in now and then. And every town has that one person.
