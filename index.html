Each tick, every burning cell tries to ignite each neighbor with P = spread × fuel factor × wind factor. Grass catches fast and burns out quickly. Big trees resist ignition but burn for a long time. Burning trees can throw embers downwind. Towns rally once fire comes within sight: crews dig firebreaks, engines spray water, and a rare air tanker drops retardant. They don't always win.
